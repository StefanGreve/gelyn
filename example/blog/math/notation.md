# Notation

A sum written inline, $\sum_{k=1}^{n} k = \frac{n(n+1)}{2}$, stays on the line it sits on. The same sum set as
display math gets its own block and its limits above and below the sign:

$$
\sum_{k=1}^{n} k = \frac{n(n+1)}{2}
$$

A limit, for the same reason:

$$
\lim_{x \to 0} \frac{\sin x}{x} = 1
$$

Dollar signs that are not math need escaping, so `\$5` renders as \$5.
